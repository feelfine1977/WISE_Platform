# 10: Evaluate a multi-criterion completion norm

Status: **proposed — owner seam/breakdown review pending; not yet a published ready-for-agent issue**.

## What to build

The analyst reviews a three-criterion Completion norm, evaluates it with real Method scope/value/coverage and inspects known absence beside criterion-local unavailability.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Reuse local draft/reviewed authoring and actual qualified Method; no parallel application scorer.
- [ ] First supported profile admits C01–C03 with exact matching-flow scope and positive criterion weights.
- [ ] Outside scope, in-scope unavailable, evaluated and unscored are separate; a bad time in one case does not block a known presence result.
- [ ] Raw/effective weights, weighted coverage, Method/norm/run identity and mandatory-control flags are visible.
- [ ] Unequal eligibility and missing timing fixtures, validation refusals and restart preserve existing Resolution behavior.
