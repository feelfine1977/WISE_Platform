# BPIC19 delivery breakdown

Status: **proposed — no new tracker issues or native edges published**. Feature branch `codex/bpic19-analysis-pipeline` is based on source `3d76a578703102d969792bc1be1ef8f272831520`.

27 new vertical slices are proposed. Each delivers a complete inspectable analyst behavior through definitions/contracts, real API, browser, evidence and durable reopening. Existing implemented foundations are reused. The final paper qualification ticket is a bounded full-workload acceptance slice, not a substitute for independent slice completion.

## Proposed numbered breakdown

1. **[Review a named source and observation policy](01-review-a-named-source-and-observation-policy.md)** — **Blocked by:** None. **Delivers:** The analyst reviews one reproduction profile, original-source identity, whole-case inclusion, raw timestamp anomalies and a trusted observation horizon, then saves/reopens that interpretation.

2. **[Profile case attributes and business grain](02-profile-case-attributes-and-business-grain.md)** — **Blocked by:** None. **Delivers:** The analyst reviews all selected source attributes by PO item, distinguishes static case values from event-varying values, verifies document/item identity and opens missing or conflicting source records before choosing process groups.

3. **[Distinguish the four purchasing flow types](03-distinguish-the-four-purchasing-flow-types.md)** — **Blocked by:** 02. **Delivers:** Before mining or scoring, the analyst selects the supplied Item Category as a reviewed process-family dimension, sees the four named groups plus unassigned/conflicting support, opens a type and keeps all original PO-item histories.

4. **[Compare flow and item-type subgroups](04-compare-flow-and-item-type-subgroups.md)** — **Blocked by:** 03. **Delivers:** The analyst subdivides a selected purchasing flow by Item Type, compares two named subgroups under the same frozen parent and opens a separate investigation with retained criteria and complete evidence.

5. **[Inspect source-witnessed activity measures](05-inspect-source-witnessed-activity-measures.md)** — **Blocked by:** None. **Delivers:** The analyst defines one presence/count/repetition measure, compares its item cohorts and opens every contributing or absent-record witness without losing full histories.

6. **[Review flow-specific analysis and completion cohorts](06-review-flow-specific-analysis-and-completion-cohorts.md)** — **Blocked by:** 01, 03, 05. **Delivers:** The analyst declares the whole-item period and recorded-completion policy for each purchasing flow, reviews closed/open-recorded/unknown and retained/excluded populations, then saves the selected cohort before comparing timing or rework.

7. **[Inspect declared milestone intervals](07-inspect-declared-milestone-intervals.md)** — **Blocked by:** None. **Delivers:** The analyst measures independent earliest-to-earliest milestone intervals under the paper policy, compares their distribution and reopens exact endpoints plus the full item history.

8. **[Inspect qualified resource proxies](08-inspect-qualified-resource-proxies.md)** — **Blocked by:** None. **Delivers:** The analyst reviews one user/batch/unknown policy and compares per-item user-event counts and distinct-user breadth with original resource records.

9. **[Review source-witnessed item-value definitions](09-review-source-witnessed-item-value-definitions.md)** — **Blocked by:** None. **Delivers:** The analyst compares a PO-creation-event value with the historical notebook’s maximum-absolute event-value proxy, inspects contributing amounts and saves the two explicitly different item measures.

10. **[Evaluate a multi-criterion completion norm](10-evaluate-a-multi-criterion-completion-norm.md)** — **Blocked by:** 03, 05. **Delivers:** The analyst reviews a three-criterion Completion norm, evaluates it with real Method scope/value/coverage and inspects known absence beside criterion-local unavailability.

11. **[Evaluate paper timing criteria with endpoint evidence](11-evaluate-paper-timing-criteria-with-endpoint-evidence.md)** — **Blocked by:** 07, 10. **Delivers:** The analyst adds the paper’s independently selected C04/C05 criteria, reads graded timing and precedence values, and opens their exact records and refusal reasons.

12. **[Evaluate paper rework and effort criteria](12-evaluate-paper-rework-and-effort-criteria.md)** — **Blocked by:** 08, 10. **Delivers:** The analyst adds C06–C10 to the admitted Completion criteria, selects the three paper views and saves a clearly identified partial profile with raw counts and attribution evidence.

13. **[Rank business groups by actual Method priority](13-rank-business-groups-by-actual-method-priority.md)** — **Blocked by:** 10. **Delivers:** The analyst ranks a declared company-by-spend-area population by count PI, sees the frozen reference and exact support, and opens the selected group’s full items.

14. **[Explain priority through signed drivers and witnesses](14-explain-priority-through-signed-drivers-and-witnesses.md)** — **Blocked by:** 13. **Delivers:** The analyst opens a priority’s signed layer/criterion explanation on an admitted run, checks coverage and selects a declared exemplar or counterexample with complete source evidence.

15. **[Compare views and ranking settings on frozen evidence](15-compare-views-and-ranking-settings-on-frozen-evidence.md)** — **Blocked by:** 13. **Delivers:** The analyst compares explicit flat/layer-balanced views and gamma/ranking policies on already admitted norm definitions, sees changed score references and shortlist membership/order, then reopens both run identities.

16. **[Inspect count priorities at document grain](16-inspect-count-priorities-at-document-grain.md)** — **Blocked by:** 02, 13. **Delivers:** The analyst groups scored source items by purchasing document, opens each document’s complete item membership and compares priorities scaled by the number of scored PO items within each document, without rescaling item scores.

17. **[Compare exact variants with a named signature](17-compare-exact-variants-with-a-named-signature.md)** — **Blocked by:** None. **Delivers:** The analyst compares one named simplified activity representation with exact repeated sequences and can return to all original occurrences and supporting items.

18. **[Inspect inferred ordinal invoice episodes](18-inspect-inferred-ordinal-invoice-episodes.md)** — **Blocked by:** 05, 07. **Delivers:** The analyst applies one reviewed ordinal episode policy, inspects its matched/unmatched events and intervals, and returns to the complete original item.

19. **[Compare FIFO-consuming invoice matches](19-compare-fifo-consuming-invoice-matches.md)** — **Blocked by:** 18. **Delivers:** The analyst evaluates an alternative FIFO-consuming match on the same retained item evidence and compares its endpoints, unmatched support and durations with the prior policy.

20. **[Review latest-preceding cancellation matching](20-review-latest-preceding-cancellation-matching.md)** — **Blocked by:** 05, 09. **Delivers:** The analyst applies one latest-preceding cancellation policy, inspects included/excluded event values and unmatched references, and saves the reproducible finding.

21. **[Explore criterion associations with explicit eligibility](21-explore-criterion-associations-with-explicit-eligibility.md)** — **Blocked by:** 10. **Delivers:** The analyst compares one target group’s criterion prevalence, severity and co-occurrence with its comparator, sees support/dependence limitations and opens affected full items.

22. **[Inspect milestone waiting occupancy over time](22-inspect-milestone-waiting-occupancy-over-time.md)** — **Blocked by:** 01, 07. **Delivers:** The analyst reviews waiting intervals and a recorded-time occupancy peak, inspects exact contributing items and saves the result with its horizon/completion policy.

23. **[Export and replay a frozen analyst pipeline](23-export-and-replay-a-frozen-analyst-pipeline.md)** — **Blocked by:** 01. **Delivers:** The analyst exports a frozen investigation report and declarative supported-action recipe, then replays/reopens it with the same definitions, evidence and current-run outputs.

24. **[Qualify the full BPIC19 paper workflow](24-qualify-the-full-bpic19-paper-workflow.md)** — **Blocked by:** 04, 06, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 23. **Delivers:** The analyst completes the paper profile on the exact full BPIC19 snapshot, reviews priorities and witnesses, exports and reopens the same result within measured capacity; notebook/report extensions retain separate receipts.

25. **[Assemble the separate historical notebook profile](25-assemble-the-separate-historical-notebook-profile.md)** — **Blocked by:** 09, 11, 12, 17. **Delivers:** The analyst loads the separately identified historical29-criterion/seven-layer/four-view profile, reviews each criterion’s actual measurement support and exports a reproducible full or explicitly partial assessment.

26. **[Check quantified recorded-event rules](26-check-quantified-recorded-event-rules.md)** — **Blocked by:** 05, 07. **Delivers:** The analyst evaluates one named occurrence-quantified precedence/response rule, opens each activation and selected partner or violation, and compares occurrence-level results with affected-item support.

27. **[Compare count and exposure-scaled priorities](27-compare-count-and-exposure-scaled-priorities.md)** — **Blocked by:** 09, 16. **Delivers:** The analyst selects a reviewed exposure calibration and compares count/exposure multipliers for item groups and documents while holding item score means and the arithmetic reference fixed.
