# 01: Review a named source and observation policy

Status: **proposed — owner seam/breakdown review pending; not yet a published ready-for-agent issue**.

## What to build

The analyst reviews one reproduction profile, original-source identity, whole-case inclusion, raw timestamp anomalies and a trusted observation horizon, then saves/reopens that interpretation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Source, raw snapshot and declared CSV/XES export counts/clock remain pinned.
- [ ] A horizon preceding raw sentinel/future dates is explicitly admitted or refused; raw histories are never silently trimmed.
- [ ] Whole-item, whole-document and named record projections have distinct exclusion/support receipts.
- [ ] Conflicting case/document/context mappings and undefined cutoff remain visible; no synthetic fallback.
- [ ] A reviewed profile with a source discrepancy and witness reopens through the primary behavior seam.
