Status: **proposed — owner seam/breakdown review pending; not yet a published ready-for-agent issue**.

## What to build

The analyst applies one latest-preceding cancellation policy, inspects included/excluded event values and unmatched references, and saves the reproducible finding.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] V1 binds cancellations to eligible latest-preceding records under explicit value/time/window/reuse rules; aggregate balance is a separate extension.
- [ ] Duplicates, credits, tolerance, zero/negative/missing values and partial matches are explicit source-witnessed states.
- [ ] Sum, average and creation-value comparisons are distinct definitions; no amount equation is chosen from a label alone.
- [ ] A declared mismatch is distinct from a proven financial breach; missing quantities/contracts remain limitations.
- [ ] Exact source totals, cancellation links, refused ambiguity and restart are checked through the analyst seam.
