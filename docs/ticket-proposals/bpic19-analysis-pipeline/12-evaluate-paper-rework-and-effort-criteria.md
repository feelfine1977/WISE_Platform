Audited source frontier: `3d76a578`. New working-tree component tests are separate from qualified public behavior; this section does not assert equivalence with a later or installed candidate.

## Source rules, product decisions and unresolved definitions

- SOURCE: C06/C07/C09/C10 apply all flows; C08 non-Consignment; repeated cycles can be legitimate and effort is a count proxy, not direct labor cost.
- SOURCE: whole-label user_[0-9]+/batch_[0-9]+ full-match, case-insensitive and outer-whitespace trim, is required on every event for both effort criteria.
- PANEL PRODUCT DECISION:8-check stage compiles no timing operators. Missing mapped activity makes a count-based conclusion unavailable; raw records remain inspectable.
- UNDEFINED BUSINESS GATE: unavoidable/justified corrections, actual handling time, staffing cost and authorized roles require evidence outside these proxies.
- PANEL PRODUCT DECISION: a known Cancel Invoice Receipt proves binary C06 presence even with another unknown activity label. Exact multiplicity and graded count availability remain separate. No generic bound engine is implied.
