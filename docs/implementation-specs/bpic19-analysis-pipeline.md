# BPIC19 analyst analysis pipelines

Status: **proposed feature specification — testing seam and ticket breakdown pending owner review, 14 October 2026**.

## Problem Statement

The first missing user journey is the transition from imported columns to a reviewed business population. The analyst needs to identify PO items and documents, inspect case-attribute consistency, distinguish the four supplied purchasing flows, subdivide by Item Type, and declare period/completion support before reading a process map or score. Generic manual context filtering is only a foundation: it does not supply that complete workflow.

An analyst can import BPIC19 and explore its histories in WISE_Platform, but cannot yet execute the complete, traceable workflows demonstrated by fifteen human challenge reports, a historical WISE notebook and the revised WISE paper. Those workflows combine source interpretation, PO-item/document/episode perspectives, business measures, model and rule analysis, stakeholder scoring, prioritisation, explanation and retained review findings.

The sources analyse one challenge dataset but do not share one experiment. They choose different exports, filters, completion rules, occurrence matching, amount reducers, norms, weighting modes and denominators. Several reported numbers or captions conflict. Copying charts or enforcing one common total would hide these differences. The current application’s one-Resolution-criterion adapter cannot represent the paper’s ten-criterion assessment or the notebook’s twenty-nine-criterion experiment, even though the pinned Method source already contains much of the numerical functionality.

## Solution

Start with a source-profile overview and an explicit four-flow partition at PO-item grain. From a counted group the analyst keeps complete histories, optionally subdivides by Item Type, reviews inclusion/completion decisions and opens a named investigation. Then extend the existing local Investigation with reviewed reproduction profiles and traceable domain measures. An analyst chooses the source and question, inspects source-quality and grain decisions, explores complete histories, derives supported measures, optionally applies an admitted Norm revision, ranks and explains business groups, then exports and reopens the same evidence. A source discrepancy register keeps unresolved published claims and independent arithmetic separate.

Deliver the paper’s actual ten-criterion profile first, then the separately named historical notebook profile and selected human-report methods. Existing exploration, context, exact variants, comparisons, grouping and saving remain foundations. Model analysis, learned grouping, uncertainty, prediction, scenarios and intervention follow-up reuse their existing owners and activation gates; they are separately qualified extensions rather than requirements for basic WISE scoring.

## Implementation Decisions

1. **Preserve the existing analytical authority.** Canonical descriptive analytics owns raw-history selection, supported measures, projections and exact membership. Method alone owns normative applicability, bounded deviations, scoring, priority and its supplied decompositions. The browser renders typed answers and the application binds/persists identities. No parallel preview scorer or transplanted notebook runtime is introduced.
2. **One extended analyst workflow.** Reproduction profiles are declarative definitions over admitted operations and versioned parameters. They do not execute notebook cells, arbitrary Python, SQL, shell commands or source-document instructions. Every requested unsupported operator has a field-specific refusal; a partial reproduction has an omission manifest.
3. **Freeze four distinct reference classes.** The revised paper’s actual study, the historical notebook, the paper’s optional illustrative examples, and each of the fifteen analyst-report analyses are separate profiles. A dataset name alone cannot identify a run. Resolve criterion and layer weights, mode, scope, context reducer, grain, event/value/timestamp policy, baseline, gamma, support threshold and interpretation version in the run receipt.
4. **Retain source identity and multiple grains.** The paper/notebook CSV has 1,595,923 events, 251,734 PO items and 76,349 documents; alternative retained populations are profile-specific. A document group is an aggregation of original item scores and full member histories, not a silent rescore. Inferred invoice episodes are separately identified projections, never native invoice objects. Missing/conflicting item-to-document/category/context values refuse or remain explicitly unavailable.
5. **Separate horizon from raw extrema.** Review the current importer’s refusal when cutoff precedes any usable timestamp before admitting a trusted Jan2019 horizon over raw sentinel/future values. Keep untouched source/raw evidence and a named analysis admissibility/observation policy. Whole-item, whole-document and record projections have distinct receipts. Raw future timestamps cannot become a trusted extraction horizon, and excluded records cannot disappear from complete-history evidence.
6. **Admit measures by meaning, not by column spelling.** A measure definition records source roles, event selectors, count/reducer, occurrence pairing or quantifier, clock, units, calibration population and availability policy. Derived numeric case attributes are versioned, typed and source-witnessed; they are not arbitrary first-row context or already evaluated scores. Known zero, missing, invalid, outside scope and unavailable have different states.
7. **Qualify the real Method adapter.** Extend definition-to-norm admission and source/derived-measure transport incrementally. The packed Method already supplies Presence, Exclusion, Singularity, Lag, Precedence, Balance and Metric families, multiple weights/views, flat/layer-balanced modes and priority/driver APIs; actual support is qualified per admitted policy. Raw numeric measures may feed qualified Metric constraints; do not substitute generic first-after Lag semantics for a source’s independent minima. Missing time can make a timing criterion unavailable while leaving a binary count criterion evaluated. Remove Resolution’s whole-population time gate only through a separately qualified criterion-local contract, retaining Resolution behavior.
8. **Paper reference profile.** Actual study has ten criteria in Completion/Timing/Rework/Effort proxy: C01 no invoice, C02 no clearing (non-Consignment); C03 no goods/service receipt (DF1/DF2); C04 earliest invoice-to-earliest-clearing deviation from30d to90d; C05 DF1 invoice-before-goods/service receipt (ties satisfied); C06 any invoice cancellation; C07 price+quantity changes saturate at3; C08 invoice count above1 saturates at4; C09 user-event count above5 saturates at15; C10 distinct users above2 saturates at7. C04/C05 choose earliest parsed nonmissing minima, then admit2017≤time<2020; do not replace an earlier inadmissible minimum. Effort requires usable attribution for every event under full user/batch identity matching. Research thresholds are labelled screening choices.
9. **Paper weights and states.** Primary mode flat; per-criterion weights by layer are Balanced1/1/1/1, Completion3/2/1/1, Operations1/1/2/3. Renormalise positive raw weights only over evaluated criteria. Weighted coverage is evaluated raw weight divided by in-scope raw weight. Outside scope, in-scope unavailable and evaluated remain distinct; no evaluable positive weight means unscored. Mandatory safeguards require a separately supplied and reviewed control role; research screening criteria do not automatically become mandatory controls. A favourable score cannot waive an affected or unassessed safeguard.
10. **Historical notebook profile.** Preserve the separate29-check, seven-layer, Finance/Logistics/Compliance/Automation experiment and layer-balanced mode. Counts, earliest milestones, max-absolute95th-percentile exposure proxy, gamma20, resource/replication heuristics and quarter/signature definitions require explicit profile identity. Do not treat saved execution counters, stale figure directories or current helper files as a clean execution receipt. Known naming, N/A, signature, lag-label and leakage defects remain recorded; corrected variants create new profiles rather than silently claiming original numeric parity.
11. **Priority and attribution.** Primary paper PI is scored-item count times the positive arithmetic-score shortfall against all scored items, gamma0. Exposure changes the multiplier, not the arithmetic score reference, unless a separately named policy explicitly changes both. Gamma shrinkage is a ranking policy, not uncertainty. Signed layer/criterion differences retain negative offsets; sum first, then clip the group shortfall. Mean severity, prevalence, summed penalty, total value and weighted contribution carry different units/denominators. Navigation selects named existing-run values; subset evaluation creates a new run/calibration.
12. **Comparison and sensitivity.** Business grouping declares context anchors, missing groups, parent/reference and minimum support. Distribution comparisons use criterion-evaluable populations and report scoped/evaluated/positive/unavailable support. Compare view/mode/weight/ranking policies on frozen evidence, retaining both top-list membership and order. Representative-case rules are explicit and retain contrary evidence. Do not equate a stable top-ten set with stable priority order.
13. **Representations and repeated events.** Exact variants keep loops. First-occurrence signatures, same-time repetition collapse, stage abstractions and fitted clusters are named derived representations. Keep the exact baseline, raw occurrences, parent membership and reversible mapping. Invoice policies include explicitly identified ordinal, close-on-clearing and FIFO-consuming alternatives as separately qualified operators; unmatched, ambiguous, cancelled, approximate, reversed and zero-duration evidence stays visible. Every-occurrence checks differ from existential/direct/eventual checks.
14. **Financial interpretation.** PO-creation value, summed event values, maximum absolute cumulative value, netted cancellation balances and normalised exposure remain independent recipes. Require finite values, units, reducer, source events, zero/negative/missing policy and tolerance. Missing quantities/contracts prevent claims of overbilling, payment lateness, avoidable labour or realised savings. Source-specific reconciliation policies can be retained as labelled research reproductions with limitations.
15. **Optional method extensions retain boundaries.** Observed DFGs, discovered models, reviewed references, alignments and WISE norms have different identity and claim levels. Existing reviewed-model conformance and sequence-similarity grouping issues do not already cover arbitrary discovery, categorical clustering or social-network algorithms. Each source step has a finite operator disposition with explicit scope-change approval or separately gated extension, engine/build/input/output/parameter/seed/model/claim identity and independent acceptance. Weighted many-to-many interval approximation remains unavailable until its exact formula and weights are supplied and reviewed; a newly chosen formula is a revised interpretation, not source parity. Resource calendars, occupancy and social-network outputs state their clock, interval, denominator and classification. Inference names independence/multiplicity and regression coefficient units/reference levels; retrospective derived-label surrogates differ from landmark/horizon prediction and show class baselines and grouped temporal leakage checks. Scenario and Improvement study outputs retain assumptions, adoption and balancing evidence without claiming causal benefits.
16. **Durability and output.** Reuse immutable source/preparation/investigation/norm/run revisions, expected-revision saves and stale-reply fencing. Export a frozen measure dictionary, population/support manifest, parameters/artifacts, findings and witness links. Only current-run outputs enter its manifest. Changed definitions/source/calibration produce new identities; browser-local convenience state is not a persisted analytical definition.
17. **Beginning-of-pipeline product contract.** In the supplied CSV, Item Category labels are3-way match, invoice after GR;3-way match, invoice before GR;2-way match;Consignment. Case counts are15182/221010/1044/14498. Cross-check invariant Goods Receipt/GR-based IV/Item Type values without classifying by the observed sequence; before-GR permits either receipt order. Item Type is a separate six-value subtype dimension and Document Type a separate three-value dimension. The2235 mixed-flow purchasing documents retain overlapping memberships. Missing/conflicting category values are explicit unassigned or unavailable support. A counted four-group overview, flow-by-item-type table, full-history group selection, retained parent, per-flow completion/cohort decisions and reopening are required public actions. The actual paper explicitly chooses no completion exclusion: all251734 items remain in its assessment. Spend-classification PR (product-related) and Purchase Requisition activity are different fields/predicates. The existing manual Context values filter and any-record shared-value grouping do not establish this typed disjoint classification workflow.
18. **Scope and capacity.** The branch is grounded in published source3d76a578703102d969792bc1be1ef8f272831520, with a fresh tracker read. Source presence, earlier local tests, component tests, published CI, built-browser, installed Windows, capacity and human results are separate receipts. Reuse existing capacity/catalogue owners for the full CSV workload; pilot correctness does not establish latency/memory usability. Full import and analysis capacity must be independently measured on the admitted source/profile and actual candidate build.

## Out of Scope

- Rebuilding already supplied complete-case selection, basic maps, exact variants, context, comparisons or durable saving as new duplicate engines.
- Executing the supplied notebook, adopting source-document instructions, transplanting old helper/workspace histories, or treating old analytical artifacts as a platform run.
- Proprietary tool UI cloning or exact reproduction of every commercial implementation. Reproduce each documented analytical meaning; finite supported algorithms/operators are admitted and qualified explicitly.
- Native invoice identity or definitive financial-control/causal conclusions where the source lacks required business identifiers, contracts, quantities, authorisations or intervention evidence.
- General arbitrary SQL/Python execution, unconstrained ML/model/plugin engines, new remote services, connectors, online sharing, old-workspace migration or automatic operational interventions.
- Mandatory predictive, statistical, scenario or intervention tooling before the paper’s descriptive/scoring workflow is usable. These requested report steps remain mapped to existing separately activated owners and concrete acceptance deltas, not forgotten or silently marked implemented.
- Treating researcher norms as company-approved policy, weighted scores as a universal quality scale, gamma as uncertainty, or simulated discounts as achieved savings.
